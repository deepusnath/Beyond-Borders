# The Launchpad: what a builder gets, when, and from whom

Beyond Borders promises more than problem statements. Once you build, a network stands behind you. This document makes that promise concrete and honest: every element is labeled **live** (works today), **informal** (exists as relationships, not yet as process), or **proposed** (designed, not yet run).

## The builder's arc

```
SIGNAL ──────────→ BUILD ──────────→ BACK
what to build      support while     the network carries
and why it         you build         you to market
matters
```

**The honest summary:** Stage 1 is real today. Stage 2 is real for anyone who shows up, informal for the rest. Stage 3 is where the promise outruns the process, and closing that gap is this repo's [roadmap](./ROADMAP.md).

## The anchor pattern

Every company entry in this repo should have an **anchor practitioner**: a named person from that company, active in the Evolve network, who has agreed to be reachable by builders working on that entry's problem statements.

The pattern already exists in embryo: **Netrasemi**, this repo's first company entry, is led by the same practitioner who leads AI Evolve. The companies behind the Space and Game chapters are natural next entries. When a chapter lead's company becomes a company entry, the signal, the mentor, and the introduction all come pre-connected. That is the launchpad's engine: **the Evolve network is the people layer of Beyond Borders.**

## What a builder must bring

The network backs contribution, not consumption. To move from Stage 1 to Stage 2: pick a problem statement and show work in progress (a repo, a prototype, a documented experiment). To move from Stage 2 to Stage 3: a working prototype and a readiness self-assessment. Nobody is graded on polish. Everybody is asked for proof of motion.

## Boundaries

- No equity is taken, no fees are charged, no exclusivity is asked. The movement's free-and-open principle applies.
- An introduction is an introduction, not an endorsement. The network opens doors; builders walk through on their own merit.
- Support follows the constitution's anti-capture rules: no company steers the problem statements toward its own sales funnel, including featured companies.
