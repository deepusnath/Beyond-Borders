# Roadmap: from map to launchpad

*The gap this roadmap closes: Stage 3 of the [Launchpad](./LAUNCHPAD.md) is promised but informal. These phases turn relationships into repeatable process, with evidence at every step.*

## Phase 0: anchor the pattern (now)

- [ ] Confirm the anchor practitioner for Netrasemi (the pattern's first case; already leads AI Evolve)
- [ ] Add **Hex20** (spacetech) and **Arc** (gaming) as company entries, anchored by the Space and Game Evolve founding leads, so every launching chapter has its company entry ready
- [ ] Add an `anchor:` field to the [company template](./company-template.md) and a Stage checklist to the [project template](./project-template.md)
- [ ] Label every existing problem statement with its vertical and difficulty, so a first-time builder can pick one in ten minutes

## Phase 1: first builders through the arc (months 1–3)

- [ ] Recruit the first cohort: 3 to 5 builders (μLearn or Evolve regulars) pick problem statements and go public with work-in-progress
- [ ] Pilot mentor matching manually: one anchor conversation per builder per month, logged briefly
- [ ] First demo slots at Evolve sessions for cohort builders
- [ ] Document every step honestly: what support was actually used, what was promised but missing

## Phase 2: make Stage 3 real (months 4–6)

- [ ] Run the first reviewed startup-readiness assessments (framework exists; add two reviewers per assessment)
- [ ] Make the first company and ecosystem introductions through a lightweight, logged process, not ad hoc favors
- [ ] Publish the first **builder journey**: one honest, named, end-to-end story from problem statement to wherever they got, including what the network failed to provide
- [ ] Grow to 6+ company entries, each with an anchor, prioritizing companies whose founders are active in Evolve chapters

## Phase 3: compound (months 7–12)

- [ ] 3 published builder journeys with evidence
- [ ] Global reach test: one builder connected to an opportunity outside India through the AI+Compassion or allied networks, documented
- [ ] Contribution loop closed: cohort builders author new problem statements from what they learned
- [ ] Review this roadmap against the movement's 90-day pilot report and the pack's evidence discipline

## What we will not do

- No demo days with judges and rankings; the readiness framework replaces theater
- No claimed partnerships or "backed by" language until an introduction has actually happened, repeatedly
- No company entry without a real relationship behind it; the catalog grows at the speed of anchors, not ambition
- No acceleration of Stage 3 promises before Phase 1 evidence exists

## The metric that matters

Not stars, not problem-statement count. **Builders who report, in their own words, that the network materially helped them after they built.** Three of those in year one beats thirty entries nobody builds from.
