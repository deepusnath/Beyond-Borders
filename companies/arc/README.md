## One-line summary

ARC is building gaming hardware from India for the Global South, with the stated mission (per co-founder Kaustubh J.) of bringing "the next one billion gamers into the fold"; per the movement's founder, it is the first game console launched from India.

## Company overview (public signals, to be validated)

From the founders' public launch (2026): over a year of building against the odds, positioned to "challenge convention, break down barriers, and make gaming and technology more accessible, not just for India, but for the Global South." Early believers include Anoop Ambika, who was also a founding enabler of μLearn's precursor group, one more thread in this ecosystem's tight weave.

The full company/technology overview should be written **with** the ARC team, not scraped about them. The anchor's validation pass is the entry's real birth.

## Why this company matters to builders here

Console hardware from Kerala is an existence proof: the full stack (industrial design, embedded systems, silicon choices, OS, content ecosystem, distribution economics) attempted locally. For Game Evolve's community, ARC turns "game development" from a software-only career into a hardware-and-platform frontier, and its Global South accessibility thesis is exactly a Beyond Borders thesis: opportunity should not depend on geography or purchasing power.
