## One-line summary

HEX20 is a payload-agnostic small-satellite company from Trivandrum building scalable satellite platforms and turnkey mission services, with flight heritage via SpaceX rideshare missions.

## Company overview

HEX20 designs and delivers small satellite platforms with modular, scalable, reconfigurable architecture, and operates across the full mission lifecycle: platforms, subsystems, payload hosting, mission-as-a-service, ground-system-as-a-service, and launch rideshare coordination. The company has launched satellites via SpaceX missions (its second satellite flew on a SpaceX launch, per the founder).

Public highlights (to be validated by the anchor):

- **DINK-N MicroSat series**: modular microsat bus, defense-standard, UHF/X-band/S-band/K-band comms, up to 100W payload power
- **Maya-V1 and NILA** platforms
- **MAGNETO**: a 12U CubeSat mission for very-low-Earth-orbit operations, mapping Earth's crustal magnetic field
- **Emirates Asteroid Impactor Mission**: HEX20 listed with TII/UAE Space Agency in a 2028–2035 mission profile to asteroid Justitia (5 billion km, three planetary flybys)
- **XORBIT**: an accelerated space program for universities, i.e., a ready-made bridge to this movement's student network

## Why this company matters to builders here

A world-class small-sat company operating from Trivandrum collapses the distance between Kerala's students and real space missions. The XORBIT university program plus Space Evolve's monthly rhythm means the path from classroom to flight-adjacent work is short and local.
